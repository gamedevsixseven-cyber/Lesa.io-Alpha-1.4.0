BIG UPDATE 1.4.0

Alpha 1.4 — Story Expansion & Boss Chapter 2!

Just Now
From: Lesa.io Team

Welcome to Alpha 1.4.0 — The Story Expansion & Boss Chapter 2! The biggest content drop in Lesa.io history expands the campaign to 20 Waves, introduces two formidable Boss encounters with interactive cinematic cutscenes, brings 2 brand-new heroes, and launches 2-Player Co-op multiplayer:

  • Chapter 2 Climax & Story Boss: Rouf (Wave 20)
    Face the village's infamous snitch! Rouf dials 100 on his 90s brick mobile phone to report you, unleashing dial-tone soundwaves ("BEEP BEEP BEEP!"), vicious close-range phone smashes, and calling squads of Greek Police Officers (Astynomos). In Phase 2, he frantically screams "STEILTE TA MAT!" calling riot-shielded MAT Police officers and 8-way soundwave bursts! Defeating Rouf triggers the cinematic ending cutscene where the Greek Police Car arrives to arrest him for 47 nuisance calls!
  • Mid-Wave Mini-Boss: Kiloumpas (Wave 15)
    The bare-chested, fiery Greek villager arrives in an interactive 2.35:1 widescreen cutscene ("Thes na parw thn karampina?" ➔ "Ferthn!"). Relentlessly snipes with his heavy Hunting Carbine rifle, and punishes close combat with a devastating 2-handed grab-and-throw hurling you 500+ pixels across the arena!
  • 2-Player Co-op Multiplayer Playtest (P2P WebRTC)
    Team up with a friend in real-time over direct peer-to-peer connection with simple 4-digit room codes! Features shared survival for both Story & Endless modes, off-screen teammate directional radar, downed ghost state, and clutch wave revives!
  • 2 New Playable Heroes: Kitsos & Billias
    Billias ("The Burger Slugger") tosses spinning burger projectiles and heals +20 HP by eating burgers with his Movement Hunger passive. Kitsos ("The Electric Speedster") is the fastest hero in the game, dropping Lightning Strikes with a 10% chance to arc chain lightning across 4 enemies!
  • Dedicated Game Mode Selection Screen
    Select between the 20-Wave Story Mode campaign, infinite scaling Endless Mode, and preview upcoming Village Dungeons with high-definition comic artwork!
  • Boss Equipment Crates & Combat Polish
    Defeating bosses now drops glowing equipment crates with randomized gear rolls. Plus, Bubble Gun secondary charged attack, enemy rename to Skatmir, and mobile performance polish!

*(Note: Dynamic environmental arena obstacles have moved to Alpha 1.5.0 alongside Epic Gear & Fusion!)*

    Added universal quick-exit buttons to all game menus, cleaned up lobby cards for claimed starter packs, and fixed several combat loop crashes for silky-smooth performance.
